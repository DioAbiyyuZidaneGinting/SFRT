About SFRT

Smart Fuel Refill Technology (SFRT) is revolutionizing the way people refuel their vehicles. Our innovative platform connects drivers with nearby gas stations, enabling seamless online fuel ordering and pickup.

Founded in 2024, we're committed to making fuel management more convenient, efficient, and accessible for everyone. With our cutting-edge technology and user-friendly interface, we're transforming the traditional gas station experience.

Our Mission

To digitize and streamline fuel management for a better tomorrow.

Our Vision

A world where refueling is smart, fast, and completely hassle-free.
